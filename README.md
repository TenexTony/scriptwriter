# Scriptwriter

Turn a rough idea or outline into a shot-ready video script built from your own spoken words.

Scriptwriter interviews you one beat at a time, captures how you actually talk, and arranges your answers into a table with dialogue, on-screen visuals, and sound notes. It supports both shortform and longform videos.

## Get the skill

The complete skill is in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). [Download the skill file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TenexTony/scriptwriter/main/SKILL.md).

Add the file to your Claude skills, then start with a prompt such as:

> Use Scriptwriter to help me turn this idea into a video script. Interview me one beat at a time and keep my own words.

## How it works

1. Choose the format, identify a specific viewer, and share an outline or talk through your idea.
2. Build and approve a beat sheet with a clear job for each beat.
3. Answer short prompts about dialogue, visuals, and sound, one beat at a time.
4. Review a script table assembled from your answers.

Your phrasing stays yours. Missing material is marked `[NEEDS: ...]`, and any permitted additions are marked `[+ ...]` so you can spot them immediately.
